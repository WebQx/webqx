🔌 Available Production APIs

  • Authentication: POST /api/auth/provider/login
  • Patient List: GET /emr/patients
  • Patient Details: GET /emr/patients/:id
  • Create Patient: POST /emr/patients
  • Update Patient: PUT /emr/patients/:id
  • Search Patients: GET /emr/patients/search?name=...
  • Transcribe Audio: POST /emr/transcribe (multipart/form-data)
  • Health Check: GET /health
  • Service Status: GET /emr/health/full
